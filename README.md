# Global Health Indicators Dashboard (2000–2025)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes global health indicators using the World Bank World Development Indicators (WDI) dataset. The dashboard was developed in Microsoft Power BI to provide interactive insights into health outcomes across countries and regions between 2000 and 2025.

---

## Project Objective

To analyze global health indicators and identify trends in:

- Life Expectancy
- Health Expenditure
- Immunization Rate
- Mortality Rate

The dashboard enables users to explore health performance across different regions and years.

---

## Dataset

**Source:**

World Bank – World Development Indicators (WDI)

https://datatopics.worldbank.org/world-development-indicators/

## Dashboard KPIs

- Average Life Expectancy
- Average Health Expenditure
- Immunization Rate
- Average Mortality Rate

---

## Key Findings

- Global life expectancy generally improved over the study period.
- Immunization coverage remained relatively high.
- Health expenditure varies across regions.
- Significant regional differences exist in life expectancy.
- Healthcare investment appears associated with better health outcomes.

---

## Recommendations

- Increase healthcare investment in lower-performing regions.
- Maintain strong immunization programs.
- Improve access to preventive healthcare.
- Reduce regional inequalities in healthcare outcomes.
- Continue monitoring health indicators for informed decision-making.
